Sample size is the total number of two-shot groups to measure. Each row in the Data Matrix records the distance between the shots in one pair. Use separate pairs such as shots 1 and 2, then shots 3 and 4.

Choose a plan you can finish that gives you a narrow enough detectable-difference band. The band is shown as a percentage around the overall average group size at your chosen risk level. More groups narrow the band and make smaller differences easier to detect. A replicate is one full pass through all combinations of the factor states.

**Example**

For a two-factor test at 10% risk, **16 groups** require 32 shots and give a band of **±31.4%**. **32 groups** require 64 shots and narrow the band to **±22%**. Submit the design to generate the rows for your chosen plan.
