The factor name identifies what changes. The two states identify the exact setups you will compare. These labels appear in the Data Matrix and in Results.

Give each factor a distinct name and enter two different state labels. Keep the labels short enough to scan while collecting data. Use the same definition of each state throughout the test.

**Examples**

- Factor **Shooting stance**. States **Weaver** and **Isosceles**.
- Factor **Rear support**. States **Rear bag** and **No rear bag**.
