The structure tells the app how many factors to compare. A factor is one thing you deliberately change, such as shooting stance or rear support. This test compares two states of each factor.

Choose one factor for a simple A/B comparison. Choose two, three, or four when you can test every combination of their states. The Data Matrix lists the combinations to use for your two-shot groups. Keep other conditions as consistent as practical.

**Examples**

- Choose **one factor** to compare Weaver and Isosceles stances.
- Choose **two factors** to compare two front rests and two rear supports. This produces four combinations, one for each pairing of a front rest with a rear support.
